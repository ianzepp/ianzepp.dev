Autobiography / selected works / 1996—present

I retired early.
Then AI made me curious again.

I’m Ian Zepp: a software founder, former engineering executive, and lifelong systems builder. This is the long version—the work, the pivots, the evidence, and what happened when a career in enterprise software collided with a year of nearly uninterrupted AI-assisted R&D.

From a KDE front end for Nmap in 2000, through Salesforce architecture and an acquired API platform, to compilers and multi-agent runtimes in 2026.

The through-line

I build systems that make other systems possible.

Platforms, compilers, APIs, engineering organizations, and now agent runtimes: the surface changes; the instinct does not.

I am drawn to the layer beneath the product—the machinery that lets a team move faster, lets software compose, or turns an ambiguous idea into something people can actually use.

The backstory

A career in four acts.

This is not a tidy ladder. It is a sequence of technical obsessions, operating companies, leadership roles, reinventions, and returns to the keyboard.

1996—2009

Learning discipline. Finding open source.

I left the Marine Corps as a Corporal and carried its bias toward responsibility into software. In 2000, I released KMap, a C++/Qt/KDE front end for Nmap—my first public proof that I could turn difficult infrastructure into a usable tool.

After years spanning real estate and independent development, I joined Beatport’s core API team as electronic music distribution was becoming real infrastructure: Zend PHP, Java, Mule, Spring, Hibernate, caching, edge delivery, and MySQL.

USMC / E-4C++ / Qt / KDEPHP / Java
2009—2017

Salesforce, scale, and the founder turn.

At Red Hat, I worked on a multilingual APAC Partner Center built on Salesforce. At roundCorner, I progressed from Chief Scientist and software architect to VP Engineering, helping turn custom nonprofit implementations into a repeatable product organization.

I then built Minted.IO: a metadata-driven platform and API business. After a year inside Salesforce as a Technical Solution Architect, Motivis acquired the Minted.IO assets and hired me to lead platform R&D.

Apex / VisualforceTeam 4 → 12Founder / acquired
2017—2024

Platform leadership, then enough.

At Motivis, I led platform engineering and R&D for a TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, and Google Cloud platform. The work continued later as independent architecture and strategy consulting through Minted Geek.

Eventually, I stepped away. Early retirement was not a sabbatical with a launch date. It was a genuine attempt to be finished with the industry—and, for a while, I was.

VP Platform R&DPaaS / GCPIndependent consulting
2025—NOW

The tools changed. The obsession returned.

Large language models did not make software trivial. They made ambitious software newly affordable to explore. I began building again—first tools, then platforms, then agent kernels, protocol bridges, security layers, and a programming language.

The result is less a collection of demos than a private research lab: thousands of conversations, hundreds of repositories, and repeated attempts to discover what human judgment plus machine throughput can build together.
